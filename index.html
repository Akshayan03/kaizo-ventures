The process

Four levels.
Clear each to advance.

Most ideas never get past the first. We would rather tell you early than build something nobody wants.

  1. I

    Pressure-test the idea

    Problem, customer, competition, willingness to pay. You leave with a go, a fix or a kill.

    To clear
    A go verdict
  2. II

    Make it real

    We audit what you built, fix security, auth and data handling, and deploy it properly.

    To clear
    Secure and live
  3. III

    Launch

    Positioning, first users and monitoring, so it holds up when real people show up.

    To clear
    Agreed milestones hit
  4. IV

    Investor introductions

    Warm referrals to angels, once you have earned them. Introductions are never for sale.

    To clear
    Final level
Who we take

Selective by design.

We take founders who
  • Have already put real time into it
  • Can name who has the problem
  • Have a prototype, or are close to one
  • Want honest feedback, including no
  • Will run the company themselves
We turn away
  • "An idea I had"
  • Solutions looking for a problem
  • Asking us to come up with the idea
  • Wanting guaranteed funding
  • Looking for a dev shop to hire
The deal

Aligned with you.

We share in the upside of what we build together. Terms are set with each founder once they clear Level I. You keep control of your company.

We promise the work.

A pressure-tested idea, a secure product that works, and a real launch.

Not miracles.

Funding, users or revenue. Nobody honest can promise those, so we will not.
